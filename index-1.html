HDW Status
HD status prep

Post sharp videos and photos to your Status.

Chat apps squash big files and blur them. HDW Status shrinks the file first, on your device, so the quality you filmed is the quality people see.

  1. 01ChooseA video or photo, vertical works best.
  2. 02EditTrim, add text and stickers.
  3. 03CompressLong clips split into parts.
  4. 04PostSave, then add from your gallery.

For the sharpest result

  • Start from the original HD file, not one that was already shared.
  • Finish all edits here. Editing again after compressing costs quality.
  • Use vertical footage so it fills the phone screen.
